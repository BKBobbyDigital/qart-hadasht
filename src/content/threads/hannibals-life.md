Hannibal Barca is the figure through whom most readers approach
Carthaginian history, and one of the figures Carthaginian history is
most thoroughly framed *around* — by the surviving Greco-Latin
sources at the time, and by every subsequent treatment that has
inherited that framing. This thread is an attempt to walk the life
through the existing encyclopedia entries in chronological order,
with editorial annotation about what to look for at each stop.

The thread cuts across two periods (the
[inter-war / Iberian project](/periods/05-interwar-and-iberian-project)
and the [Second Punic War](/periods/06-second-punic-war)) and ends in
the [long peace](/periods/07-the-long-peace). It also
crosses the editorial line where the encyclopedia takes its strongest
position about the war's outcome — the
[Cannae editorial take](/editorialTakes/cannae-roman-refusal-to-negotiate)
appears as stop 9 deliberately, at the moment in the chronology where
the question becomes unavoidable.

The thread complements the long-form narratives
[Young Hannibal](/narratives/young-hannibal) and
[Hannibal after Zama](/narratives/hannibal-after-zama), which are the
site's continuous prose treatments of the early and late phases of
the life. Use the thread as a tour of the surviving evidence and the
narratives as the connecting framework.
